## Highest impact

1. **Body font is not Medium-like.** Everything is Poppins, including 20px essay text. Medium reads
   well because the body is a serif (Charter / Source Serif) and only the UI is sans. Suggest
   `--font-body: "Source Serif 4", Charter, Georgia, serif` for `.content`, keep a sans for UI.
   Poppins is geometric and tiring over long essays such as *Meditation*.
2. **Homepage does too many jobs.** Stats strip (14 posts / 7 journals / 14 topics) tells a reader
   nothing; "Explore Topics" shows topics with 1 post; the sidebar has three cards (Explore More,
   Quick Links, Popular Posts) that repeat the navbar and each other. Medium's home is just the feed.
   Suggest: one-line intro with photo + latest writings + "Now" snippet; drop the stats and Quick Links.
3. **Out-of-date self-descriptions conflict with the CV.**
   - Home sidebar "About Me": "Computer Science student passionate about…" (generic, old).
   - About page "Experience" list (Braincore 2024–2025, Len 2023…) and `/timeline/` are older,
     shorter versions of the CV. Replace both with a link to `/cv/` or delete `/timeline/`.
   - `/now/` was last updated Apr 2026.

  title/subtitle; the meta line wraps ("7 min read" on its own line).

- "More ▾" now holds 6 items. Bookshelf, Uses, Fun Facts, TIL, Timeline live only in the home
  sidebar, so they are hard to find from any other page. Consider one `/more/` index page.
